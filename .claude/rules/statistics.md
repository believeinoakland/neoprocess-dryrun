# Statistics: the area's rules

1. A statistic is computed with Arithmetic's operations, never with its own addition or rounding (canon §1).
2. An empty list is judged before any element (canon §2).
3. The midpoint of two numbers is Arithmetic's midpoint operation, never a sum halved or two halves summed (canon §3; Arithmetic §4; L-STATISTICS-1).
